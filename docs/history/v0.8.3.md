> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Upgrade notes.** Back up `config/` before upgrading and keep the existing
> `data/` mount. Existing v0.8.1 and v0.8.2 databases advance from schema 066
> through numbered migrations 067–068; fresh installs use
> `baseline/068_v0.8.3.sql`. Older databases continue through the numbered
> migration chain; do not replace an existing database with a fresh-install
> baseline.
>
> Migration 067 adds a per-account time zone setting and migration 068 adds
> the tables that count listening per hour of the day. Neither rewrites
> existing rows: listening history, per-day totals, and playback progress are
> kept as they are.
>
> **Listening days follow a time zone.** Each account counts its listening
> days in the time zone of the device in use, or in one zone chosen in
> Settings → Account. A client that names no zone is counted in the server's
> zone: the container's `TZ` when it names an IANA zone, and UTC otherwise.
> Listening recorded before the upgrade keeps the day it was counted on, and
> the hour-of-day chart starts empty because earlier listening has no hours.
>
> **Listening history moved to Insights.** The History tab is no longer in
> Settings. `/history` and `/settings?tab=history` redirect to **Insights** in
> the sidebar.

> [!NOTE]
> Use `docker compose up -d --pull always` to upgrade the production stack;
> restarting a container reuses its installed image. Update `KIKOTO_IMAGE`
> first if it is pinned to an older release. Update the iOS app as well to
> receive the blank-screenshot protection.

Kikoto v0.8.3 adds **Insights**, a page for an account's own listening with a
yearly calendar, year reports, a taste profile, and a listening rhythm counted
in the account's time zone. Circles and Voice Actors are redesigned around
cover tiles and sortable works, the Library gains Full, Compact, and Mini
cards on every screen, and a work opens inside the page it was opened from.
The metadata editor gains fixed language tabs, a tag list that follows the
selected language, and **Fetch for edit**, which compares fetched metadata
with the current values before anything is saved.

<details>
<summary><strong>Library and work detail</strong></summary>

- A work opens inside the page it was opened from. Opening a work from
  Favorites, Circles, or Voice Actors keeps that destination selected in the
  navigation, Back returns to it, and on phones each bottom tab resumes its own
  open work. Tapping the selected tab again returns to its list. A work address
  opened directly belongs to Library. On phones, double-tapping the Full
  player's cover returns to the tab the playback started from.
- Library display options replace the phone-only **Compact cards** switch with
  a **Cards** choice of **Full**, **Compact**, and **Mini**, available on
  phones and wide screens and remembered separately for each. Compact on wide
  screens uses short tiles, and Mini shows only the cover and title so a large
  library can be scanned by its artwork. A device that had Compact cards on
  keeps compact phone cards.
- Local and Tracked can show 96 works per page.
- On wide screens the Library sort control is one button that names the
  current order and its direction. Its popover holds the orders and the
  direction, and the Recommended and Random orders keep a refresh button
  beside it.
- The Favorites resource filter offers All, Available, Local, Remote, and
  Missing in a narrower popover, in place of the cached and per-source
  choices. It follows the availability each card shows, so a work whose files
  belong to another language edition, or whose local folder has no indexed
  tracks, is filtered as Local instead of Missing. The grid or list view moved
  into display options.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Circles and voice actors</strong></summary>

- **Circles** and **Voice Actors** list each creator as a cover tile with an
  availability bar for local, remote-only, and missing works, and show the
  latest release date in place of the latest work code. Their views are
  one-tap chips beside the search field, which stays visible on a phone.
- Circle detail opens with a cover-tinted summary of its **Available**,
  **Local**, **Remote**, and **Unavailable** counts, and a circle that was
  never pulled says that only works the library already knows are listed.
- Circle and voice actor detail order their works by release date, code,
  title, rating, or sales, offer the **Full**, **Compact**, and **Mini** cards
  of the Library, and can show 96 works per page. The order and page size are
  remembered on the device. Voice actor works follow the chosen order instead
  of listing local works first.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Insights and listening</strong></summary>

- **Insights** is a sidebar page for the account's own listening. It opens
  with a calendar of the past year, keeps the 30-day, 12-month, and all-time
  listening report, and ranks tags, voices, and circles by preference (share
  of listening time over share of the Library) or by listening time. Listening
  history moved here from Settings; `/history` and `/settings?tab=history`
  redirect to it. Reports count listening per Library work, so listening
  stored against an edition appears under its work.
- Insights reports a calendar year as well as the named ranges, and a year
  opens with its highlights: the work, voice, and circle listened to most, the
  busiest day, the longest streak, and the works first heard that year.
- A taste profile compares the listening with the Library: the tags sought
  out most as a radar, where the listened works sit by release year, sales,
  and length, and how the account's ratings compare with the public ones.
- Listening is counted per hour of the day and shown as a chart in the
  listening rhythm. Hours start with the upgrade; earlier listening has none.
- A time zone decides where a listening day starts. Each account follows the
  zone of the device in use, or chooses one zone for every device in
  Settings → Account; Insights names the zone and links to the setting.
  Listening recorded before the upgrade, or before a time zone change,
  keeps the day it was counted on.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Metadata and scans</strong></summary>

- **Fetch for edit** in the metadata editor fetches the work's metadata from
  DLsite or a metadata-capable remote source into a private comparison with
  **Current**, **Fetched**, and **Expected save** columns. Nothing is written
  until **Save selection**. Using a fetched value accepts the provider
  metadata and removes an older manual override, editing the expected value
  saves a manual override, and a current value kept beside an accepted
  fetched field is pinned so the provider update cannot replace it.
- The comparison can merge the current and fetched tags, and **Accept fetched
  family** is a separate action: saving ordinary fields never adds sibling
  editions to the library. The preview needs `metadata:sync`, is kept for a
  short time, and cannot be applied after the work has changed.
- The metadata editor's language tabs list **All languages**, Japanese,
  Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, and Korean for every work.
  **More** opens the work's editions in other languages and names the chosen
  one, such as `More - German`. The Tags section lists the work's tags one per
  row and follows the selected language: each row shows the tag's name in that
  language with its other names beneath, and its language button opens that
  language's name inside the row.
- DLsite metadata synchronization accepts sparse numeric-keyed
  `language_editions` responses as well as arrays. Comma-separated language
  values are matched to their labeled edition so affected work families can
  synchronize normally.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Apps and device privacy</strong></summary>

- With **Hide from screenshots and recordings** on, a screenshot taken in the
  iOS app shows a blank page in place of Kikoto. The setting replaces **Hide
  while the screen is recorded or mirrored** and keeps the stored choice.
- The media controls choice under **Privacy on this device** is a segmented
  control that shows its three options at once.

</details>
